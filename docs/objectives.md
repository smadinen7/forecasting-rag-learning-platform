# Learning Objectives

This document defines five measurable learning objectives using Bloom's taxonomy verbs. Each objective builds progressively from foundational knowledge to higher-order synthesis and evaluation.

## 1. **Identify** (Remember/Understand)

**Objective:** Learners will **identify** at least three types of structural breaks (e.g., level shifts, trend breaks, volatility regime changes) and describe their typical causes in corporate finance contexts (e.g., regulatory changes, market crashes, technology disruptions).

**Assessment:** Multiple-choice quiz or short-answer questions asking learners to classify break types from time-series plots or case descriptions.

---

## 2. **Apply** (Apply)

**Objective:** Learners will **apply** standard statistical tests (e.g., Chow test, CUSUM, Bai-Perron) to historical financial datasets to detect structural breaks, interpreting test outputs and setting appropriate significance thresholds.

**Assessment:** Hands-on coding exercise or case study where learners run break detection on provided data and justify their chosen test and threshold.

---

## 3. **Analyze** (Analyze)

**Objective:** Learners will **analyze** the trade-offs between real-time and retrospective break detection methods, evaluating false-positive rates, detection lags, and computational costs in operational forecasting scenarios.

**Assessment:** Written analysis comparing at least two detection approaches on a benchmark dataset, with quantitative metrics and qualitative discussion.

---

## 4. **Synthesize** (Create/Evaluate)

**Objective:** Learners will **synthesize** a forecasting pipeline that integrates break detection with adaptive overlays (e.g., regime-switching models or ensemble methods), demonstrating improved forecast accuracy or robustness in a simulated break scenario.

**Assessment:** Capstone project where learners build and document a working prototype, presenting results with before/after performance metrics.

---

## 5. **Evaluate** (Evaluate)

**Objective:** Learners will **evaluate** the effectiveness of their forecasting system using break-aware metrics (e.g., regime-conditional RMSE, break-adjusted MAE) and reflect on governance implications (model monitoring, stakeholder communication, ethical considerations).

**Assessment:** Reflective essay or presentation analyzing system performance, metric choices, limitations, and recommendations for production deployment and continuous improvement.

---

**Bloom's Taxonomy Alignment:**
- **Level 1-2:** Identify (Remember/Understand)
- **Level 3:** Apply (Apply)
- **Level 4:** Analyze (Analyze)
- **Level 5:** Synthesize (Create/Evaluate)
- **Level 6:** Evaluate (Evaluate)

**Assignment Mapping:**  
Objectives align with Steps 1–2 and guide the design of modules, RAG queries, and evaluation criteria (Steps 4–6).
